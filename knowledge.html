Meridian Knowledge

Making sustainability easier to understand.

Sustainability has developed its own language — ESG, materiality, reporting, governance, stakeholder engagement, transition plans and more. Meridian translates these concepts into practical business understanding.

Why Knowledge Matters

The challenge isn't finding information. It's knowing what to do with it.

The sustainability landscape is filled with frameworks, standards, regulations, reports and competing interpretations. But information alone does not create understanding. Meridian Knowledge is designed to provide the context behind the terminology — helping readers understand what matters, why it matters and how the pieces connect.

The Meridian Regional Lens

Global knowledge. Southeast Asian reality.

A sustainability framework may be global, but its implications are often local. Meridian examines global developments through the realities of Southeast Asian markets — with particular attention to Indonesia, regulation, institutions, sectors and stakeholders.
