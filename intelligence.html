The Intelligence Layer

Information tells you what happened. Intelligence helps you understand why.

There is no shortage of sustainability information. The challenge is separating important signals from background noise — and understanding what those signals mean for strategy, risk, governance and business decisions.

Why it matters

Why it matters

The Meridian approach is deliberately simple: identify what is changing, understand why it matters, and translate it into implications that decision-makers can use.
