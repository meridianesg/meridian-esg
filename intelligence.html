REGULATION
01 · REGULATORY SIGNAL

The rules are becoming harder to ignore.

Sustainability expectations are increasingly moving from voluntary commitments toward more structured regulatory and disclosure requirements.

Why it matters

Regulatory change can affect governance responsibilities, reporting systems, data quality and ultimately business risk.

02 · MARKET SIGNAL

Investors are asking better questions.

Environmental, social and governance issues increasingly intersect with resilience, risk, capital allocation and long-term enterprise value.

Why it matters

The challenge is not to report everything. It is to understand which issues can genuinely influence decisions.
