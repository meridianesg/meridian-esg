About Meridian

Experience changes the way information is interpreted.

Meridian ESG was created from a simple observation: organisations do not suffer from a lack of sustainability information. They suffer from having too much of it — and too little context.

Our perspective is shaped by more than two decades working across corporate affairs, CSR, sustainability, government relations, community development, international organisations and governance.

The objective is not to add more noise. It is to help people understand what matters, connect the dots and make better decisions.
