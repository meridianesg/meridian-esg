The Meridian Method

Five questions can change the conversation.

Good navigation starts by asking better questions. These five questions sit behind much of the way Meridian looks at sustainability and responsible business.

01

What matters?

Separate what is interesting from what could genuinely matter.

02

Why does it matter?

Connect the issue to business, people, risk, opportunity and the wider context.

03

What is changing?

Look beyond today's position and understand where expectations and conditions are moving.

04

What should you do?

Turn understanding into choices, priorities and action.

05

What are others getting wrong?

Sometimes the most useful insight comes from seeing the blind spots.

A Meridian observation

The hardest sustainability problems rarely arrive labelled “ESG”.

They may look like a community issue, a supply-chain problem, a regulatory concern, a reputation risk or a business decision. Understanding the connection is often where the real work begins.
