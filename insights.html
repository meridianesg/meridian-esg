Featured Perspective

A good sustainability report can still hide a bad sustainability strategy.

Reporting quality and sustainability performance are not the same thing. An organisation can produce an impressive report while the underlying strategy remains fragmented, poorly governed or disconnected from business decisions.

The better question is not: “How good is the report?”

It is: “What changed because of it?”
